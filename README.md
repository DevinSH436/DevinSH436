## 💫 About Me:

M.Sc. Computer Science student (AI & Machine Learning) at the **University of Windsor** and AI/ML engineer with hands-on experience building
machine learning, LLM and AI-agent applications. I've built a natural-language-to-SQL engine with security guardrails at **Altametrics**,
sales-forecasting models that cut error from **~12–15% MAPE to 5%** at **ANR Software**, and ETL + Power BI automation at **PVSOFT**.
I enjoy turning research ideas and new AI tech into practical, reliable software.

- 🌱 Interested in: Generative AI & LLMs · AI agents & multi-agent systems · NLP · Computer vision · MLOps
- 💼 Open to: AI/ML engineering, Generative AI, software engineering and research roles in Canada (remote friendly)
